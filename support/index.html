Getting Started

Distill turns bestselling books into short audio clips you can scroll through. Download the app, create a free account, and start learning from books right away!

A subscription is required to access the app which can be purchased at any time.

02

Subscription & Billing

How do I cancel my subscription?
On iPhone: go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Distill → Cancel. On Android: go to Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Distill → Cancel.
How do I restore a previous purchase?
Open Distill, go to Settings inside the "Me" tab, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase.
I was charged but can't access content. What do I do?
Try restoring your purchase first (see above). If the issue persists, email us at support@distillit.app with your order details.
03

Account

How do I delete my account?
Email us at support@distillit.app with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete your personal data within 7 days of confirmation.
I can't sign in. What should I do?
Make sure you're using the same sign-in method (Apple or Google) you used when you first created your account. If the issue continues, contact us and we'll help.
